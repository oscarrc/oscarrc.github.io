# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Two audiences in equal measure: fellow developers who discovered Oscar through an app or library and want a hub for the rest of his work, and recruiters/clients evaluating him professionally. Both arrive briefly, scan, and click out — the page is a launcher, not a destination. No contact section for now.

### Brand Personality
Precise, quiet, engineered. The page should feel like a well-machined tool: confident without shouting, technical without costume. Emotional goal: instant credibility through restraint.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark only — no light theme. Reference: Vercel / v0. Near-black tinted surfaces, monochrome hierarchy where the "accent" is brightness (off-white) rather than a loud hue. Elegance through subtraction: hairline borders, generous space, one or two deliberate details. Anti-references: neon-on-dark "AI dashboard" looks, glassmorphism, gradient text, decorated cards.

### Design Principles
1. **Monochrome discipline** — hierarchy comes from lightness steps and type weight, not color. Tint all neutrals toward one hue (cool violet-blue, chroma ≤ 0.01).
2. **The work is the interface** — project tiles are the page's protagonists; everything else (name, labels, footer) recedes.
3. **Hairlines and air** — 1px borders, generous whitespace, no shadows-as-decoration.
4. **One screen, no theater** — a single composed section; motion limited to one orchestrated entrance and quiet hover feedback.
5. **Typography over ornament** — Schibsted Grotesk for identity and names, Spline Sans Mono strictly for micro-labels (section eyebrows, copyright).
